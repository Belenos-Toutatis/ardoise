# Wacom Inkspace App: Feature Research (desktop 3.x, Windows/Mac, with Bamboo Slate / Folio / Spark)

**Research status:** Partial. Wacom's official Inkspace desktop 3.x manual and support articles could not be retrieved (wacom.com product page returned 404, support.wacom.com section returned 403, Microsoft Store and Apple App Store pages returned title-only or 404). Most entries below come from search-result summaries of Wacom product/marketing pages, app-store listings and a 2016 Wacom news item. Anything not confirmed in those sources is marked **(unconfirmed)**. No feature has been added beyond what a source mentioned.

**Key caveat:** Search results describe the Inkspace app mainly as a mobile/web app. Whether each feature exists in the **desktop 3.x Windows/Mac** build is not confirmed for most items. A Wacom community post reports that Mac support was discontinued with no reintroduction date (user report, not an official statement, unverified).

**Legend:** "Inkspace Plus (paid)" is the marking requested. Sources describe Plus as a *free* upgrade tier ("Upgrade to the new free Plus plan"), so no feature is confirmed as paid. Items below are marked "Plus" where a source ties them to the Plus tier, with paid status (unconfirmed).
